About The Venue

An Architectural Marvel in Central Delhi

Talkatora Stadium is a prominent multi-purpose indoor stadium located in the serene Talkatora Gardens, near the President's Estate.

Built with unique architectural elements, the stadium is fully air-conditioned and celebrated for its commitment to sustainability, integrating eco-friendly measures like rainwater harvesting and solar panels. With a seating capacity of around 3,000, it offers an intimate yet grand atmosphere for any event.

Athletics & Competition

A Legacy of Sports

Historic Venue

Originally constructed for the 1982 Asian Games, the indoor stadium has built a rich and lasting legacy as a premier hub for indoor sports development in India.

Indoor Competitions

Regularly hosts national and international boxing championships, badminton tournaments, wrestling matches, and table tennis. Notable athletes, including Mary Kom, have trained here.

Outdoor Facilities

The adjacent Talkatora Cricket Ground complements the indoor arena, offering practice pitches, nets, and basic gym equipment for cricket matches and robust training programs.
